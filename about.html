Our Story

A focused bridge between producers and buyers

Orient Welt was founded in Hamburg to support reliable food trading between Middle Eastern supply regions and European customers.

Our work centers on frozen packed vegetables, with an emphasis on steady communication, clear expectations, and careful product selection.

As the company grows, we continue to build relationships with producers, distributors, and food-service buyers who value dependable cooperation.

Mission & Values

Reliable trade, practical service, lasting trust

Our mission is to make international frozen vegetable trading more dependable for producers and buyers through disciplined sourcing and responsive service.
